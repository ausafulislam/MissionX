# Scientific accuracy and limitations

## External references

NASA's [Design and Develop Science Missions](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/250323-design-and-develop-science-mission.pdf?emrc=a454cf), sections “What segments make up a science mission?” and “Big Picture Process,” is explicitly cited for conceptual mission segments/requirements. The four result segments are LAUNCH/SPACE/GROUND/USER. This relationship is not proof of historical method adoption, copied coefficients or validation. Mission-page references provide science themes, not hardware equivalence.

GMAT, JPL CML, MDL, Mission Viz, Webb game, CubeSat101, Space Apps2023 VR and CSA activity links are scoped educational/professional comparisons. No imported NASA code, API, data feed, GMAT solver or real orbital propagation is established. [Classifications/sections/rights](../docs/RESOURCES.md).

## Authored educational model

Catalog masses, prices, lifetimes, power/battery/data capacities, orbit factors, reliability, event probabilities, science points and score policy are MissionX-authored estimates. Original balancing rationales are not recorded. [Every numeric parameter record](../docs/DATA_SOURCES.md) and [formulas/units](../docs/ENGINEERING_REFERENCES.md) separate implementation evidence from scientific accuracy.

Operations use four representative end-of-life days,48 steps/orbit, synthetic eclipse/contact schedules, finite energy/memory and stationary capped-lifetime extrapolation. No independent convergence study or physical validation is claimed. Geometry, altitude and travel time are schematic; Sun Watch observes the Sun from Earth orbit.

## Diagnostics and outcomes

PASS means a configured check/presence test passed, not measured spacecraft health. Propulsion/thermal, real insertion/navigation, RF antenna/link acquisition and calibration remain NOT EVALUATED. Reviewing six groups is an interaction gate, not certification. Weighted score rounding is separate from mission outcome;100 does not guarantee every requirement passed.

Snapshots preserve recorded inputs/results, not a historical executable or signed measurement. Snapshotless records use disclosed current-catalog reconstruction. Current visual telemetry/explanatory metadata are not wholly archived.

## Unvalidated areas

No thermal/structural/propulsion/RF solver, discoveries/coverage prediction, real vendor pricing, NASA validation/endorsement, professional flight readiness or classroom efficacy study. Physical-device GPU/audio, screen-reader/PDF accessibility and broader browser/print sign-off remain manual. See [full limitations](../docs/Scientific-Limitations.md).
