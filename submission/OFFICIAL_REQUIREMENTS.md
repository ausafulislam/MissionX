## Verified requirements and human checks

- Project page must answer required form fields: project name, high-level summary, demo link, final-project link, detailed description, SI use, NASA resources and other references. Do not assume these Markdown documents automatically populate the form.
- Demo is a **slide presentation of at most7 slides including title/intro OR a video at most30 seconds**. English is required for submission/linked materials; non-English spoken video requires English subtitles. A3–5-minute live walkthrough is not the official short demo.
- Demo and final links must be publicly accessible without password, registration or permissions. Images may be uploaded (JPG/PNG/GIF up to10MB); videos/documents must be externally linked. Convert/review repository WebP screenshots if uploading to the form; do not assume WebP upload support.
- Global Award eligibility requires at least one NASA data/resource. MissionX explicitly references NASA mission-segment/requirements concepts, not numerical NASA data. Owner must describe that use accurately and confirm eligibility with organizers rather than upgrading it to an adopted method or guaranteed eligibility.
- Universal Event global-judging eligibility requires2–6 members registered for that same event and present in the team Members tab. Exact roster/acceptance is owner confirmation. No member names/roles are invented here.
- Guide says submission opens November 14 at09:00 and closes November 15 at23:59; **Universal Event uses the Team Owner's residence timezone**. Confirm owner residence/timezone and live form; Karachi team location alone is not proof of owner timezone. Local judging can differ.
- Terms/privacy and original-work/permission attestations are required. The guide forbids unpermitted copyrighted content and requires references for code/text/images even when open source. **Unresolved art rights prevent an honest blanket permission attestation.**
- SI assistance is permitted with conditions. Guide requires disclosure of where/how used; generated images/video require visible watermarks, audio descriptive/metadata acknowledgement, code/data descriptive/metadata acknowledgement. It prohibits generated NASA branding/design elements and says SI should not originate the challenge-solution idea. Owner must verify full creation history, including idea origin and any generated artwork; do not infer compliance from tool use being allowed.
- Project demos/final projects may not include likeness/audio/video of persons under18 per the submission guide. Owner checks recordings/assets and full legal terms; no legal assessment performed here.
- Click **Submit for Judging** before deadline; saving progress is insufficient. Confirm success banner/message, review the final saved submission and retain confirmation privately.

## Five evaluation criteria

Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance and Presentation are named by the guide. Describe learning intent and demonstrated functionality; no user study, award, measured learning improvement or ranking is established.
