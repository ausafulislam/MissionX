# MissionX — Design. Simulate. Learn.

**Team:** NASA WARRIORS · **Version:**0.1.12  
**Challenge:** Space Mission Design Game · **2026 event theme:** The Next Frontier

## High-level project summary draft

MissionX is a browser-based educational game that turns space mission design into a sequence of connected decisions. Players choose Moon Mapping, Sun Watch or Mars Orbiter, assemble a spacecraft, balance mass, budget, power and data, verify their configuration, and evaluate a seeded mission. A procedural 3D headquarters and semantic flight-deck alternative connect the experience to six arrival configuration reviews and a downloadable report explaining outcomes and limitations.

The intended audience is students, educators and curious beginners. MissionX makes resource trade-offs visible so a stronger instrument can also be seen as a mass, power, storage and cost decision. Its educational value is an intended benefit, not a measured learning-outcome claim.

## Current status

The complete single-player loop, catalogs, resource engine, seeded events, diagnostic/report projections, local history and PDF export are implemented and tested locally. Optional remote AI advice has a deterministic fallback; no live provider verification is claimed. There is no cloud multiplayer, GMAT, NASA API integration or real trajectory propagation.

New-run records capture authored inputs and results; legacy inputs are disclosed as reconstructed when absent. The weighted score is independent of outcome rounding:100 is not a guarantee that all objectives passed.

Public final submission still needs owner review of artwork rights/SI compliance, team eligibility, the short demo, production revision/public links, tooling advisory and official attestations.
