# Community code of conduct

MissionX welcomes students, educators and contributors of all backgrounds. Be respectful, patient and constructive. Critique ideas and evidence rather than people; make technical explanations accessible and acknowledge uncertainty.

## Expected behavior

- Use considerate language, respect boundaries and credit others' work.
- Respect privacy and consent; do not share personal information or private saves.
- Support newcomers and keep discussion relevant to the project.
- Label scientific uncertainty honestly and avoid false affiliation/endorsement claims.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, impersonation, spam and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. This applies to repository issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces, including people acting on the project's behalf.

## Reporting and enforcement

No private conduct-reporting contact is currently published. Do not post sensitive incidents or personal information in public issues. The owner must establish a confidential reporting channel; the Space Apps site's general contact is not a MissionX moderation channel.

Repository maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, restrict participation or block accounts using available platform controls. Responses should consider severity, context, privacy and conflicts of interest; a warning is not required before addressing serious harm. No response-time guarantee or formal appeals process is currently established. The owner must publish reporting/enforcement contacts before claiming a complete private reporting process.

This is a project-specific policy, not a claim to have adopted an unmodified external code-of-conduct standard.
